Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Pawbi makes smart NFC and QR tags for pet collars. When someone finds a pet, they scan the tag and see how to reach the owner. This page explains what information Pawbi handles to make that work, and the choices you have.

  • We collect only what the service needs: your account, your pet’s profile, and reports finders send you.
  • Your pet’s page is public. Anyone who scans the tag sees what you put on it.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can edit what finders see, clear your alerts, and ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. What we collect

Your account. Your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication, so we never see or store your password ourselves. You can also save a phone number to pre-fill new pet profiles.

Your pet’s profile. Everything you choose to add: name, photo, animal type, gender, breed, color, age, personality and care notes, microchip number, vet details, your own extra details, and your contact details (name, phone, backup phone, email).

Tag activity. Which tag belongs to which account, how many times a tag was scanned, and when it was last scanned. We do not record who scanned it.

Found reports. When a finder taps “I found this pet”, they can share their location, a short note and a phone number. We save these and show them to the pet’s owner. Pawbi administrators can also access them to run the service.

Phone alerts. If you turn on alerts, we store the technical details your browser gives us to send notifications to that device (a push subscription).

Technical data. Our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, as every website does. To stop spam, we also keep a scrambled (hashed) value made from a finder’s IP address and the tag, which cannot be turned back into the IP address.

2. What finders can see

Anyone who taps or scans your tag can see your pet’s page without logging in. It shows the details you added, and always shows your main phone number so a finder can reach you. You decide whether your name, email, backup phone and WhatsApp button are shown. Anything you hide is not sent to the finder’s device. We never ask for your home address, so please do not put it on the page.

3. Location

We only receive a location when a finder taps “Share my location” and allows it in their browser. We save the coordinates with the report and show them to the owner. We do not track anyone’s location in the background.

4. How we use your information

  • To run your account, show your pet’s page, and let finders contact you.
  • To send you notifications when someone reports your pet.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, such as limiting repeated reports and activation attempts.
  • To answer your questions and fix problems.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We rely on these providers to operate Pawbi:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in and for storing accounts, pet profiles, tags and reports.
  • Vercel for hosting the website and running our server functions.
  • Google Fonts and Google’s content servers, which deliver the site’s font and app code. Your browser connects to them directly, so they receive your IP address.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare), which delivers the library that draws QR codes when you download or print one.
  • Browser push services run by Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, which deliver notifications to your device.

Some buttons open other services only when you tap them, such as WhatsApp and Google Maps. Their own privacy policies apply from that point.

6. Cookies and device storage

Pawbi does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay logged in, and the app briefly remembers which page you wanted to reach while you log in.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and pet profile: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Found reports: until you clear them from your dashboard, or your account is deleted.
  • Phone alert subscriptions: until you turn alerts off, log out, or the device stops accepting notifications.
  • Anti-spam values: kept in hashed form for as long as needed to limit abuse.

8. Your choices

  • Edit your pet’s profile at any time. Changes show up instantly on the tag’s page.
  • Hide your name, email, backup phone or WhatsApp button from finders.
  • Clear found alerts from your dashboard, one at a time or all together.
  • Turn phone alerts off in Settings or on your device.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information, including your whole account, by emailing [your contact email]. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Pet profiles and reports are only readable by their owner and administrators, and tag activation codes are stored in hashed form. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Children

Pawbi is meant for pet owners aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you think a child has given us their information, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Where data is processed

Our providers run servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app or by email.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email [your contact email].
